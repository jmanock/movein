# Future Internet deals strategy

MoveIn does not currently publish deals, coupon codes, affiliate links, advertisements, or sponsored placements. `/internet/deals` is intentionally absent rather than exposing a thin “coming soon” page.

## Data foundation

Provider records reserve optional fields for offer title, description, destination, offer type, validity dates, promo code, affiliate status, disclosure, and last verification date. Empty fields produce no UI.

## Verification standard

A future promotion must come from an official provider or documented commercial feed, apply to a known geography or address result, show its verification date, include material conditions, and be removed or hidden when expired. MoveIn should never infer an offer from old marketing copy.

## Possible offer types

- Provider promotions
- Verified coupon codes
- Installation discounts
- New-customer offers
- Equipment promotions
- Clearly disclosed affiliate offers
- Clearly separated sponsored placements

## Neutrality boundary

Paid status must never add a provider to availability results, change provider ordering, suppress an alternative, change relationship confidence, or create a “best” recommendation. Organic availability and paid offer modules must use separate data and presentation.

## Launch gate

Do not launch a public deals route until at least two current offers can be verified, expiration handling is automated, disclosures are approved, outbound tracking is tested, and organic comparison results remain unchanged with commercial data disabled.
