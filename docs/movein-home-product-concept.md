# MoveIn Home product concept

Status: future concept only — not implemented

## User problem

The move ends, but household knowledge remains scattered: maintenance dates, filter sizes, alarm checks, appliance records, warranties, insurance files, emergency contacts, and shared responsibilities. My Move solves the short transition; a future MoveIn Home could support the years after it.

## Free and paid boundary

Always free: ZIP lookup, My Move basic checklist, First 30 Days, printables, moving guides, address updates, utility guidance, and basic local resources.

Potential paid value: cross-device sync, household sharing, recurring maintenance reminders, scheduled calendars, secure document storage, warranty tracking, private home inventory, appliance records, and longer-term home history. Existing free content must never be moved behind a paywall.

## Possible pricing hypotheses

Only test pricing after repeat usage exists. Future research could compare a low annual household plan, a modest monthly plan, and a one-time first-year home setup product. These are hypotheses, not offers; no price should be published before demand, support cost, security scope, and retention are understood.

## Recurring value and retention loops

- HVAC and water-filter reminders based on user-entered equipment guidance.
- Smoke/CO alarm, dryer-vent, hurricane-preparation, and seasonal checks.
- Warranty, insurance-renewal, and service reminders.
- Household maintenance calendar and shared completion history.
- Secure appliance, emergency-contact, and document retrieval when needed.

## Privacy and security concerns

Cross-device sync changes the risk profile materially. Exact addresses, home inventories, access information, documents, warranties, serial numbers, insurance records, and household sharing require explicit consent, encryption, authentication, retention controls, export/deletion, abuse prevention, vendor review, incident response, and a clear data-minimization policy. Do not reuse the local-only My Move model as if it were sufficient for cloud storage.

## Potential future affiliate opportunities

Only after editorial trust and demand exist, MoveIn could evaluate clearly disclosed referrals for moving supplies, insurance education, home monitoring, filters, or qualified service marketplaces. Paid relationships must never alter provider rankings or official-source guidance. No affiliate feature is part of the current product.

## Metrics required before building

- Meaningful repeat visits over several months.
- My Move creation, return, task-completion, and reset rates.
- First 30 Days and printable engagement, including print intent.
- Evidence that users return after the moving week.
- Qualitative demand for reminders, sharing, inventory, and document storage.
- A credible security budget, support model, acquisition cost, retention forecast, and willingness-to-pay signal.

Do not build payments, subscriptions, accounts, or cloud storage until these thresholds and risks are reviewed explicitly.
