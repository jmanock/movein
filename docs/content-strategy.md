# Content strategy

MoveIn organizes content around real decisions after someone receives the keys. The core sequence is timeline first, audience hub second, focused guide third, and official or qualified source when a decision needs current or individual guidance.

## Priority topic clusters

- Moving and checklists: move-in, new homeowner, renter, first week, and first month.
- Homeowner confidence: safety, maintenance, systems, records, and emergency preparation.
- Renter protection: condition documentation, lease responsibilities, utilities, safety, and deposit records.
- Florida: moving, hurricane preparation, flood context, homestead research, vehicles, tolls, utilities, HOAs, pests, seasonal maintenance, and emergency resources.

Each substantive guide must include a concise answer, visible steps with reasons, a relevant timeline stage, related guides, a review date, editorial attribution, a correction path, and a general-information disclaimer. Changing Florida facts should link to primary state, county, federal, or utility sources.

## Duplication safeguards

- Do not create separate pages for singular/plural or lightly reordered keyword variants.
- Merge overlapping topics and use permanent redirects from retired slugs.
- Do not create city or county pages from a template. A local page needs maintained local contacts, official resources, and genuinely distinct guidance.
- Campaign pages are acquisition surfaces, not a scalable SEO template. They remain noindexed and canonicalize to the useful destination.
- Do not pad pages, repeat introductions, hide keyword text, or add unsupported FAQ content.

## Editorial review

Review safety, emergency, insurance, tax, vehicle, and regional content before lower-impact lifestyle material. Prefer official sources, remove obsolete hardcoded fees or deadlines, and record meaningful updates. Corrections go to `hello@movein.guide`.
