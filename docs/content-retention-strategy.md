# Content and retention strategy

Last reviewed: August 10, 2026

MoveIn now connects organic entry pages to useful repeat behavior without accounts or monetization.

## Retention loop

1. A visitor lands on a utility, renter, safety, ZIP, or moving-reminder page.
2. A contextual action adds the relevant task to My Move.
3. My Move organizes it around the move date and audience while local provider context remains clearly provisional.
4. First 30 Days and the printable library provide broader planning and offline use.
5. A deterministic “Don’t Forget This” reminder offers one quiet next step without a popup.

## Search-intent boundaries

`/resources/things-people-forget-when-moving` owns the broad missed-task intent. Existing address, utility-transfer, first-week, safety, and renter-condition pages keep their specific intents. `/first-30-days` is a compact navigation and checklist hub, not another long generic article. Printables are standalone tools, not thin download pages.

ZIP lookup, My Move, First 30 Days, guides, printables, and basic local resources remain free. This sprint adds no ads, affiliates, email capture, account system, subscription, payment code, contractor marketplace, or state expansion.

Use aggregate page, add-task, completion-category, print, reminder, guide-to-lookup, and return-visit signals. Do not join checklist behavior to personally identifying data. Major product changes should pause until a comparable Search Console and GA4 review.
