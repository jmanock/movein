# SEO strategy

MoveIn targets high-intent questions after a move: finding possible utilities, understanding who is responsible, and completing the next official action. The search architecture is **hub → guide → reviewed ZIP result → official source**, with trust pages available from every route.

## Principles

1. Publish for a real user task, not a location-keyword permutation.
2. Answer the question early, then explain uncertainty and the exact next action.
3. Treat ZIP data as a shortlist. Water, sewer, trash, and internet often require a complete-address check.
4. Index only pages with reviewed, sourced, actionable value.
5. Keep one canonical URL per purpose. Campaign and parameter variants must redirect or remain noindex rather than become copies.
6. Prefer official provider, city, county, state, and federal sources; show review dates.

## Deferred templates

State, county, and city templates are not launched in this release. They become eligible only with unique local context, at least one reviewed official source, meaningful service guidance, distinct metadata/body copy, breadcrumb and internal-link support, and an explicit indexability approval. “Move Score” and site-search/SearchAction schema are also deferred because the product has neither a defensible scoring methodology nor a general site-search feature.

## Bing and IndexNow

Submit the canonical sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools after domain verification. IndexNow is optional and intentionally not enabled: this is a small, stable site and no securely managed IndexNow key or high-frequency publishing workflow exists yet.
