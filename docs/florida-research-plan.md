# Florida research plan

The current pilot covers 12 representative ZIPs across Seminole, Orange, Volusia, Lake, and Osceola counties. Expansion follows the generated queue in `data/florida/research-queue.csv`.

Priority is electricity first; water and sewer second; trash third; internet provider/technology evidence fourth; natural gas fifth; and additional jurisdiction detail last. Review exact-address or parcel tools when available and store unresolved overlaps as research notes rather than selecting a winner.

Near-term work:

1. Resolve electric territories for Sanford, Lake Mary, Apopka, DeLand, Daytona Beach, and Clermont.
2. Verify Leesburg water, sewer, and trash sources and Orlando/Kissimmee trash arrangements.
3. Add address-supported internet provider and technology observations without speeds, prices, rankings, or affiliate links.
4. Research gas territories across all 12 ZIPs without inferring service from a county-level map.
5. Add the next population centers: Altamonte Springs, Oviedo, Deltona, Port Orange, Tavares, Eustis, and Celebration.

Generate a fresh queue after each reviewed import. A task leaves the queue only when a non-lookup provider record exists for that category.
