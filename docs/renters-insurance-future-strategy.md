# Future renters-insurance strategy

MoveIn does not currently compare insurers, collect quote information, recommend carriers, rank coverage, or earn insurance commissions. The existing renter guide is educational and links to official consumer sources.

## Neutral comparison standard

A future `/renters/insurance` experience should separate general education from any provider directory. Organic ordering must be neutral, availability must be supported by current official evidence, and payment must never change eligibility, ordering, or editorial conclusions. MoveIn should not label an insurer “best,” infer suitability, or reproduce a quote as though it applies to another visitor.

## Compliance and regulatory review

Insurance marketing, referrals, producer licensing, quote collection, endorsements, and compensation can trigger state-specific requirements. Before any commercial launch, MoveIn should obtain qualified compliance review for every state served, confirm what activities require a license, document insurer and intermediary roles, and establish a process for reviewing changing requirements. Educational copy must remain clearly distinguishable from individualized insurance advice.

## Privacy boundary

The current site must not collect a name, email, phone number, date of birth, street address, property details, claim history, policy details, or requested coverage limits for insurance purposes. A future quote or lead workflow would require a separate data-minimization, consent, retention, security, vendor, deletion, and incident-response review before implementation.

## Commercial possibilities

Future options might include clearly labeled affiliate links, licensed referral relationships, or sponsorship separated from organic information. Every paid link would need an adjacent disclosure, an auditable destination, current commercial terms, and analytics that exclude quote answers and personal data. Lead generation should be rejected if MoveIn cannot explain where the data goes, why each field is necessary, how long it is kept, and how the visitor can exercise applicable privacy rights.

## Launch gate

Do not launch commercial insurance features until legal and compliance review is complete, privacy documentation and contracts are approved, provider evidence is current, paid and organic presentation are technically separated, accessibility and security testing pass, and MoveIn can remove expired or noncompliant relationships promptly.
